Missed a sentence? Tap it to hear it again.

Spend your time practicing, not scrubbing.

When a whole lesson is a single track, going back one sentence means hunting along the timeline with your finger. Reprise is built to remove that step.

Automatic sentence splitting

On-device speech recognition transcribes the audio and splits it by sentence, phrase, or chunk (2–5 word groups). If a boundary is off, merge, re-split, or fine-tune it against the waveform.

Playback made for practice

0.5×–2.0× speed with pitch preserved, single-sentence loop, and stop-after-each-sentence. A “same length as the phrase” pause leaves just enough time to say it back. Hide the text to focus on listening.

Everything stays on your device

Your audio, transcripts, and study history are never sent to a server, and there's no account to create.

Three steps

  1. Import

    Tap “+” and pick your audio. A short built-in tutorial lets you try it right away.

  2. Split

    Analysis runs on your device and takes a few seconds to a few minutes, depending on length.

  3. Tap a sentence

    Play just that one. Combine loop, pause, and speed to listen, shadow, and repeat.
